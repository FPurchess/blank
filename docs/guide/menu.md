# The main menu

Everything Blank can do is in one menu, behind the logo at the top left. Click it, or press `Mod` `K` wherever you are.

<Shot src="main-menu.png" alt="The main menu below the logo: a search field, Recent, the File commands with Open recent and Export, rows for Edit, View, Zoom and Theme, and Settings, Keyboard shortcuts, Guide and About Blank" />

## Search for a command {#search}

The menu opens with the cursor in its search field. Type a few letters of what you want, like _pdf_, _table_ or _dark_, and Blank lists the commands that match, with their group and their shortcut. The first one is ready: press `Enter` to run it, or move to another with `↑` and `↓`. Among equally good matches, the commands you used recently come first.

The search finds every command, also the ones that aren't in the menu itself, like inserting a table or setting the language. `Esc` clears what you typed, and a second `Esc` closes the menu.

<Shot src="main-menu-search.gif" alt="Mod K opens the main menu; typing pages lists Pages / page ends first, with its group and shortcut, and Enter switches the view" />

## What's in it {#contents}

- **Recent**: the last few commands you ran, once there are some. It leaves out what the menu shows anyway, and typing like _Bold_ done with its shortcut.
- **File**: new, open and save, **Open recent** with the last ten files you opened or saved, **Export** as PDF or Word, and [**Print**](./print). A recent file that has moved or was deleted is taken off the list when you try to open it, and _Clear list_ empties it.
- **Edit**: undo, redo and [find and replace](./find).
- **View**: the blocks pane, the [outline](./pages#outline), [pages or page ends](./pages#on-the-screen) and [focus mode](./writing#focus-mode). The menu stays open, so you can see the switch change; the blocks pane opens with the cursor in its search.
- **Zoom**: smaller, the [zoom](./pages#zoom) (a click fits the pages to the window again) and larger.
- **Theme**: a swatch for each [theme](./themes), in its own colors.
- **Settings** opens the [settings](./settings), **Keyboard shortcuts** and **About Blank** open them on that section, and **Guide** opens this guide in your browser.

## With the keyboard {#keyboard}

`↓` in the empty search field goes into the menu at its top, `↑` at its bottom, and `↑` `↓` move through it; `↑` on the first line goes back to the search. In a row like View, `←` and `→` move between its buttons. `→` or `Enter` opens Open recent and Export, and `←` closes them again. When you start typing in the menu itself, the letters go to the search. `Mod` `K` while the menu is open brings you back to the search.

The logo is also part of the row of tabs: press `F6` to reach the tabs, then `Home` or `←` on the first tab to get to the logo, and `Enter` opens the menu.
