# Settings

Press `Mod` `,` to open the settings. Everything you change applies at once, in every tab, and stays after a restart: there is nothing to save and no need to start Blank again. Close them with **Close** or `Esc`.

The sections are listed on the left; `↑` and `↓` move between them, and the settings open again on the section you saw last.

## Appearance

- **Theme:** click a card to switch to that [theme](./themes). Each card shows the theme's own colors.
- **In focus mode, hide the controls:** when [focus mode](./writing#focus-mode) lets the bars fade. They always fade when you type; choose whether they also fade after the mouse has rested for 3 or 10 seconds.

## Writing

- **Autocorrect:** one switch for each group of [autocorrect](./autocorrect), from arrows and dashes to links.
- **Your replacements:** click **Edit…** to add text that Blank replaces for you, such as `btw` → `by the way`, for every language or only the current one. `Esc` or **Back** brings you back.
- **Indent size:** how many spaces `Tab` indents a line of a [code block](./writing#code) by, from 1 to 16.

## Spelling

- **Check spelling** turns [spell check](./spelling) on or off, like `Mod` `Alt` `S`.
- **Language** chooses the language of spell check and autocorrect, like `Mod` `Alt` `L`.
- **Ignore words in capitals** and **Ignore words with numbers** leave words like _NASA_ or _mp3_ alone. The text is checked again as soon as you change them.
- **Your dictionary:** click **Edit…** to see the words you added in the current language, add new ones and remove others. Underlines go and come at once.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Every command, with its shortcut. Search for one, click its shortcut and press the new keys. See [Change a shortcut](./shortcuts#change-a-shortcut).

## About

Blank's version, its license, links to the website and the source code, and the licenses of the software Blank is built with.

## Where the settings are kept

The theme, the language and whether spell check is on are kept with your documents. Everything else is written to [blank.json](./configuration), which only holds what you changed. You can still edit that file by hand; then restart Blank to use what you wrote.
