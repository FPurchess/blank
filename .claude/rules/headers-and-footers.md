# Headers, footers and page numbers

- They are page settings (`src/layout/settings.ts`), in the `page` key of the frontmatter like the rest:
  - `header`/`footer`: `left`/`center`/`right` slots of one line each
  - `first-page`: `same`, `plain` (none) or its own `header`/`footer`
  - `even-pages`: the header and footer of even pages
  - `number-style`: `1`, `i` or `I`
  - `start-number`
  
  `BAND_KEYS`/`bandSettings` are these keys; the page setup dialog keeps them as they are, and **Make this my default** saves only the dialog's keys.
- The slots hold plain text with the fields of `src/layout/tokens.ts` (`{page}`, `{pages}`, `{title}`, `{author}`, `{chapter}`, `{date}`, `{file}`; `{{` for a brace). There is no formatting, on purpose.
- `src/layout/bands.ts` is what both exporters share:
  - the band's size and distance from the edge (`BAND`, `BAND_ROOM`)
  - `bandVariant` and `bandsOn`: which bands a page has, and those bands. The first page's own or none; even pages by the number they show, as in Word.
  - `variantsOf`, `formatNumber` (roman numerals), `documentFields`, `fieldValues`. `{chapter}` is found by the engine itself, per page. `src/layout/bands.parity.test.ts` checks that the engine's bands are `bandsOn` + `fieldValues` + `expand` for the number styles, start numbers, first and even pages, so change both sides together.
- The pages and the PDF: the layout engine lays out the bands of each page (`src-tauri/layout/src/bands.rs`, a port of `bands.ts` and `tokens.ts`) after it paginated, so `{chapter}` and `{pages}` are known; the screen paints them on the sheets and the PDF holds them.
- A placeholder that comes out empty on a page ({author} with no author set, {chapter} before the first heading 1, {title}, {file}) is named on the screen only, so the band can still be seen and opened: `src/layout/placeholders.ts` splits each slot into text and such placeholders (`slotParts`, `pageBandParts`). Page ends show the name as an `em.band-placeholder` (`src/ui/BandSlots.vue` in the marks of `PageFrame.vue`), and the sheets in the `.page-band-names` overlay over the painted band. The PDF and Word print the text alone and stay empty there.
  - `hasBand` follows what is written in the templates, not what comes out, so a band whose placeholders are all empty keeps its room on the screen and its click target.
  - `emptyBandNotice` runs when the strip's change is applied (`tellIfEmpty` in `src/editor/commands/editBand.ts`): if the band comes out empty on that page although something is written, the status line says which placeholders and why, and how to set the author.
  - `FIELD_NAMES` names the placeholders as the strip's insert buttons and the slots' chips do (`INSERTS` in `src/ui/bandStripsModel.ts`, `chip` in `src/slotEditor.ts`). `FIELD_FULL_NAMES` next to it are the longer names, which screen readers read for the chips and the insert buttons' tooltips use ("Page number", "File name"). `bandSlots` takes the header's or footer's three of a page's six slots.
  - `sheetSlots` (`src/ui/pageViewModel.ts`) places the overlay's names where the engine sets the band: it repeats `band_boxes` in `src-tauri/layout/src/engine/display.rs` (each slot a third of the text width; the header `BAND_DISTANCE`, 36 pt, below the top edge; the footer at the bottom margin, moved down by what the margin leaves beyond 36 pt and one band line; `BAND_SIZE` 8.8 pt with line height 1.3, `BAND_LINE`, in `src-tauri/layout/src/bands.rs`, which `BAND` in `src/layout/bands.ts` mirrors). `bandBox` there is that box, which `sheetSlots`, the band targets on the sheets and the slots being edited share. A change to that geometry must change both; `src/ui/sheetSlots.parity.test.ts` checks that the engine's band text lies in the slots `sheetSlots` gives.
- Word (`src/exporters/docx/bands.ts`):
  - One paragraph per band in Word's `Header`/`Footer` style, with center and right tab stops.
  - Word's own fields: `PAGE`, `NUMPAGES`, `TITLE`, `AUTHOR`, `STYLEREF "Heading 1"`, `DATE \@ "<picture>"` (`datePicture`) and `FILENAME`. Their names and the page number formats are in `fields.ts`, which the Word import reads them back by.
  - `first` and `even` parts, `titlePage`, `evenAndOddHeaderAndFooters` and `pgNumType`.
  - A band without text anywhere gets no parts, and every part has a paragraph.
- Word import (`src/importers/docx/bands.ts`):
  - It reads the parts of the first section back into slots, by their tabs, `w:ptab` and `w:jc`.
  - It descends into `w:sdt` content controls, where Word's page number gallery puts its fields.
  - It maps the fields back, and keeps the shown text of other fields.
  - It warns about pictures, tables, several lines and number styles Blank lacks.
- A header or footer is edited on the page where it prints, not in the dialog (`src/ui/BandEditor.vue`, mounted by `BandStrips.vue`, keyed by its request):
  - **At rest** the page view shows the bands: on the sheets in "pages", and in "page ends" above the first page, where each page ends, and below the last one (`PageFrame.vue`, `PageEdgeBand.vue`). Each is a `BandTarget.vue`, a button out of the tab order that a click opens on its page: outlined (dashed `--line-strong`) under the pointer, with the tooltip "Edit the footer" and its key. While the document has no such band at all (`shownAtRest`), it's the hint "+ Header" or "+ Footer" instead, which shows while the pointer is on the sheet's margin, the mark's header or footer row, or the room above the first page and below the last (`edgeHintPlace`). Above the first page in "page ends" that room is small, and the properties line covers it where there is one, until the properties line goes. A double click anywhere in a sheet's top or bottom margin opens the band too. There is no hint for page numbers alone: the strip's **Page number** puts them in, and the command search finds the footer by "page numbers". The hints should hide in focus mode, once it exists.
  - **Which page:** `editBand(band, page?)` (`src/editor/commands/editBand.ts`) opens the band of the page clicked, or of the page in view (`pageInView` in `src/engine/geometry.ts`, the page the bottom bar counts) for the keys and the command search; none without pages. `openStrip` then shows the band that page has (`bandVariant`): the first page's own on page 1, the even pages' on an even page, else every page's. `tellIfEmpty` checks that page.
  - **Where:** the strip's root `#band-editor` (`role="group"`, named "Header" or "Footer") lies over the page view and clips what it holds, so it never covers the bars; it takes no clicks itself. The slots (`.band-slots`) go over the band, a little larger (`slotRowPlace`), and the strip (`.band-card`, on the popover surface) beside them: below a header, above a footer, or on the other side where the view has no room, as wide as the sheet and inside the view and the window (`stripPlace`). `bandEditorPlace` (`src/ui/pageViewModel.ts`) gives both from the view's corner, and whether the slots come first. `bandInWindow` (`src/ui/bandStripsModel.ts`) gets the band from `bandPlace` (the sheet's band or the "page ends" line) through `framesNow` and `deskToWindow`, so they follow the scrolling, the window and the zoom without listeners of their own. The keys open it with `center`: the view then centers the band (`centerRequest`) unless the band and its strip already show (`fitsInView`); a click opens it where it is, so a double click's second press lands in its slots. Without pages (no engine), the strip sits at the window's top or bottom edge (`.at-edge`) and holds the slots itself. While it's open, the pages fade and take no clicks (`body.band-editing`), but the view still scrolls; a drag of its scrollbar counts as a press outside and closes the strip.
  - **On the pages:** `pageBands` (what a page's six slots show), `addsBand` (whether to offer "+ Header"/"+ Footer") and `openBandOn` (open a page's band, as a click does) in `src/ui/bandStripsModel.ts` serve `PageFrame.vue`, `PageEdgeBand.vue` and `BandTarget.vue`; `sheetTargets` places the targets in a sheet's margins. The page view leaves the selection alone on a press on them (`BANDS` in `PageView.vue`: `.page-band, .band-target, .band-hint`).
  - **What it holds**, in `src/bandStrip.ts` without its DOM (`openStrip`, the functions that change it, its menus, `bandSummary`, and `stripSettings`, what it keeps):
    - the head: the band's icon and name, on which pages it is, **Remove** (only its icon turns red under the pointer, since the themes' red is too faint as text) and **Done**;
    - **Insert**: **Page number** (a `MenuButton` with the presets, the numbering and **Start at…**) and the placeholders of `INSERTS`;
    - **Pages**: **First page** (a select `MenuButton`), the switch **Odd and even pages differ** (`SwitchControl`), and **Mirror the odd pages** while it's on. Mirroring is an action, not a setting: turning even pages on starts them as the odd ones mirrored (`toggleEvenPages`), and the button mirrors again (`mirrorOddPages`); the file keeps the even pages' own band;
    - **Editing**: the pages whose band the slots show (`SegmentedTabs`), while there are more than one.
  - **Keys:** Tab goes around the controls and slots in the order they show (the slots first where they are above the strip, as for a header, last where they are below it; `slotsFirst` of `bandEditorPlace`), leaving out the tabs a roving row keeps out of the tab order; Esc on a control, Enter or Esc in a slot, Done, and a press outside the strip, its slots and its menus keep what was typed. It's a focus-taking dialog (`focusTakingDialogs`), not an F6 stop. A change through a control keeps the focus there if the keys made it, else gives it to the slot last in use.
  - `openBand` publishes a `bandEditor` request, whose `apply` writes all band settings as one undo step and gives the editor the focus back. Opening it says "Editing the footer", Remove "Footer removed", and Mirror "Even pages mirrored".
  - `src/slotEditor.ts` is the small ProseMirror editor of one slot (`role="textbox"`, named "Footer, left" and so on), with the placeholders as atom chips that show their name and read as their longer one; `SlotField.vue` mounts one and leaves its DOM to ProseMirror, and a change of the pages shown mounts new ones (their key counts those changes).
  - The bands at rest show what `pageLayout` resolves and `pageFields` fills in (`src/state/page.ts`). Both stay the same while typing leaves the frontmatter, the first heading and the file alone, so they render again only then.
