Keyboard shortcuts ​

Mod is Cmd on macOS and Ctrl on Windows and Linux. All shortcuts except the line break and Mod + Click can be changed.

Files ​

CommandShortcut
New fileMod N
Open fileMod O
SaveMod S
Save asMod Shift S
Export as PDFMod Alt P
Cycle themesMod Alt T
Choose languageMod Alt L

Editing ​

CommandShortcut
UndoMod Z
RedoMod Shift Z
Insert line breakMod Enter or Shift Enter

Blocks ​

CommandShortcut
ParagraphMod 0
Heading 1 – 6Mod 1 … 6
Bullet listMod 8
Numbered listMod 9
Indent list itemTab
Outdent list itemShift Tab
BlockquoteMod G
Horizontal lineMod H

Text ​

CommandShortcut
BoldMod B
ItalicMod I
CodeMod E
Insert or edit linkMod K
Open link in browserMod + Click

You can also create most of these by typing markdown. See Format as you type.
