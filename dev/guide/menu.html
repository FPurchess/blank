On this page

The main menu ​

Everything Blank can do is in one menu, behind the logo at the top left. Click it, or press Mod K wherever you are.

Search for a command ​

The menu opens with the cursor in its search field. Type a few letters of what you want, like pdf, table or dark, and Blank lists the commands that match, with their group and their shortcut. The first one is ready: press Enter to run it, or move to another with ↑ and ↓. Among equally good matches, the commands you used recently come first.

The search finds every command, also the ones that aren't in the menu itself, like inserting a table or setting the language. Esc clears what you typed, and a second Esc closes the menu.

What's in it ​

  • Recent: the last few commands you ran, once there are some. It leaves out what the menu shows anyway, and typing like Bold done with its shortcut.
  • File: new, open and save, Open recent with the last ten files you opened or saved, Export as PDF or Word, and Print. A recent file that has moved or was deleted is taken off the list when you try to open it, and Clear list empties it.
  • Edit: undo, redo and find and replace.
  • View: the blocks pane, the outline, pages or page ends and focus mode. The menu stays open, so you can see the switch change; the blocks pane opens with the cursor in its search.
  • Zoom: smaller, the zoom (a click fits the pages to the window again) and larger.
  • Theme: a swatch for each theme, in its own colors.
  • Settings opens the settings, Keyboard shortcuts and About Blank open them on that section, and Guide opens this guide in your browser.

With the keyboard ​

↓ in the empty search field goes into the menu at its top, ↑ at its bottom, and ↑ ↓ move through it; ↑ on the first line goes back to the search. In a row like View, ← and → move between its buttons. → or Enter opens Open recent and Export, and ← closes them again. When you start typing in the menu itself, the letters go to the search. Mod K while the menu is open brings you back to the search.

The logo is also part of the row of tabs: press F6 to reach the tabs, then Home or ← on the first tab to get to the logo, and Enter opens the menu.
