# On Writing

Good writing starts with a blank page and a single idea. Everything else — the layout, the typography, the export — should stay out of your way.

## Why keyboard only?

> Your hands stay on the keyboard, so your mind stays on the sentence.

- Headings, lists and quotes are one shortcut away
- Autocorrect sets “quotes”, dashes and arrows → as you type
- Export to PDF when you are done
